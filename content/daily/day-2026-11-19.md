# Thursday, November 19 — Day 50

**Every circle hides the same number: π ≈ 3.14.**

Read this first (about **8 minutes**). It builds on yesterday's triangles and Day 33 (around vs. inside).

## The parts of a circle

- **Radius:** from the center to the edge.
- **Diameter:** all the way across, through the center. **Diameter = 2 × radius.**
- **Circumference:** the distance around.

## π

Measure any circle. Its circumference is always a bit more than **3 times** its diameter. That number is **π** ("pi"), about **3.14**.

## Around: circumference

**Circumference = π × diameter**, or **2 × π × radius**.

- Diameter 10 cm: 3.14 × 10 = **31.4 cm**.
- Radius 5 cm: 2 × 3.14 × 5 = **31.4 cm**. Same circle!

## Inside: area

**Area = π × radius × radius**.

- Radius 3 cm: 3 × 3 = 9, then × 3.14 = **28.26 cm²**.

**Watch out:** area uses the **radius**, not the diameter. If you're given a diameter, halve it first.

| Want | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Circumference | 3.14 × diameter (or 2 × 3.14 × radius) |
| Area | 3.14 × radius × radius |

## World — the Gettysburg Address

Reading time: about **11 minutes**. Every World question on the exam comes from here.

**163 years ago today, on November 19, 1863, Abraham Lincoln gave the Gettysburg Address.**

### The Civil War

In 1861, **11 Southern states** broke away and formed the **Confederacy**, with its capital in **Richmond, Virginia**. The main cause was **slavery**: the Confederacy's own leaders said they left to protect it. The North (the Union), led by President Lincoln, fought to keep the country together.

In 1863, Lincoln's **Emancipation Proclamation** declared enslaved people in the rebelling states **free**. Nearly 200,000 Black soldiers joined the Union army.

### Gettysburg

In July 1863, the biggest battle of the war was fought at Gettysburg, **Pennsylvania**. In three days, **about 50,000** soldiers were killed, wounded, or missing.

### The speech

Months later, at the opening of a cemetery there, the main speaker talked for 2 hours. Then Lincoln spoke for **about 2 minutes, around 270 words**. It began:

> "**Four score and seven years ago** our fathers brought forth on this continent, a new nation, conceived in Liberty, and dedicated to the proposition that all men are created equal."

A **score is 20**, so four score and seven is **87** years, back to 1776. Lincoln said the war was a test of whether "**government of the people, by the people, for the people**", a **democracy** where ordinary people rule, could survive.

The Union won in 1865, and the **13th Amendment** ended slavery in the whole country.
