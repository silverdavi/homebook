## Why a review week

You've covered a lot: GCF, LCM, all four fraction operations,
multiplication and the breakdown trick, the periodic table, valence,
fifteen wars, twelve peace accords, the whole evolution timeline.
Covering something once is not the same as knowing it. This week is
about turning "I saw that" into "I know that."

## Where the points have been leaking

Three honest spots from the last three weeks:

1. **Valence.** You're usually one or two off. That's a counting
   problem, not a knowledge problem — read the **group number**, don't
   count chairs across the row. The valence lesson now has eight
   practice questions with answers. Do them.
2. **Two-digit multiplication.** Your breakdown method is right; the
   arithmetic slips (you subtract the wrong amount). Slow down on the
   last step.
3. **Peace-accord dates from memory.** When you use notes you're
   nearly perfect. Westphalia is **1648**, not 1638 — that one has cost
   you three times.

## A bigger point about history

You know "Oslo is Israel and the PLO." Good — but that's half. The
other half is **what it tried to do and why it failed**: it was meant
to lead to a Palestinian state, and instead settlements grew, Rabin
was killed, and no state came. A date with a story attached sticks. A
date alone does not. The peace lesson has the one-line story for each
accord — read those, not just the years.
