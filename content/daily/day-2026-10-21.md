## Perimeter means around

Perimeter is the distance around the outside edge.

For a rectangle that is 4 cm long and 3 cm wide, add all 4 sides:

**4 + 3 + 4 + 3 = 14 cm**

You can also use **2 × length + 2 × width**. It means exactly the same thing.

## Area means inside

Area counts how many square units cover the inside.

A 4-by-3 rectangle has 4 columns and 3 rows:

**4 × 3 = 12 cm²**

Area is **length × width**. It is not length divided by width.

## Keep the questions separate

For a 5 cm by 2 cm rectangle:

- perimeter: 5 + 2 + 5 + 2 = **14 cm**
- area: 5 × 2 = **10 cm²**

Ordinary **cm** means a line or distance. **cm²** means squares covering a surface.

Today has only rectangles. There are no triangle or volume questions. The exam uses only the same two ideas taught and practiced here.

## World block — biomes and climate justice

This part takes about **11 minutes**.

- **Tundra:** cold, treeless, short growing season; much Arctic tundra has permafrost.
- **Desert:** very little precipitation. A desert can be hot or cold.
- **Rainforest:** warm and wet, with extraordinary biodiversity.
- **Grassland:** grasses dominate; rainfall is seasonal or too limited for dense forest.

Biomes describe patterns, not walls. They blend into one another, and humans have shaped them for thousands of years.

Climate change does not affect everyone equally. Communities that contributed least to greenhouse-gas emissions are often among the most exposed to drought, flooding, heat, and food insecurity. That is one meaning of **climate justice**.

Indigenous peoples have long managed forests, grasslands, and water systems. Conservation works better when it respects their knowledge and land rights; conservation has also caused harm when governments expelled people from ancestral lands.
