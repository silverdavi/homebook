**Density tells you how tightly matter is packed.**

The complete clock is above. This reading takes about **9 minutes**.

## The three quantities

- **mass**: how much matter, measured here in grams
- **volume**: how much space, measured here in cm³
- **density**: mass in each unit of volume

## Density

**density = mass ÷ volume**

A block has mass 60 g and volume 20 cm³.

60 ÷ 20 = **3 g/cm³**

## Find mass

**mass = density × volume**

Density 4 g/cm³ and volume 6 cm³:

4 × 6 = **24 g**

## Find volume

**volume = mass ÷ density**

Mass 45 g and density 5 g/cm³:

45 ÷ 5 = **9 cm³**

Write the unit. A bare 3 is incomplete; **3 g/cm³** says what the number means.
