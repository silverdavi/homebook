## Communication

If something is confusing or missing, say exactly what it is. Raging instead of communicating starts a chain reaction of hurtful exchanges and delays the fix.

Home school gives you more freedom and easier discipline. It also requires more independence and clear communication.

## Perimeter: around

For a rectangle:

**perimeter = 2 × length + 2 × width**

A 9 cm by 4 cm rectangle has perimeter:

18 + 8 = **26 cm**

## Area: inside the flat shape

Rectangle:

**area = length × width**

9 × 4 = **36 cm²**

Triangle:

**area = base × height ÷ 2**

A triangle with base 10 cm and height 6 cm has area:

10 × 6 ÷ 2 = **30 cm²**

## Volume: inside a box

**volume = length × width × height**

A 5 cm by 4 cm by 3 cm box has volume:

5 × 4 × 3 = **60 cm³**

Units reveal the question: cm is length, cm² is area, cm³ is volume.

## World block — maps, flags, and power

This part takes about **10 minutes**.

- **Japan** is in Asia. Its flag has a red circle on white.
- **Brazil** is in South America. Its flag is green with a yellow diamond and blue globe.
- **Canada** is in North America. Its flag has a red maple leaf.
- **South Africa** is in Africa. Its flag has a sideways green Y.

The equator divides the Northern and Southern Hemispheres. Continents and oceans are physical geography; national borders are human decisions.

Maps are useful models, not neutral photographs. A Mercator map makes places near the poles look too large. Borders can be changed by migration, negotiation, war, and empire. Many borders in Africa were drawn by European colonial governments with little regard for the people already living there.

The point is not to distrust every map. It is to ask: **Who made this map, for what purpose, and what does it emphasize or leave out?**
