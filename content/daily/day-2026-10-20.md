# Tuesday, October 20 — Day 33

**Measure the boundary, the surface, or the space.**

The complete clock is above. This reading takes about **10 minutes**.

## Perimeter: around

For a rectangle:

**perimeter = 2 × length + 2 × width**

A 9 cm by 4 cm rectangle has perimeter:

18 + 8 = **26 cm**

## Area: inside the flat shape

Rectangle:

**area = length × width**

9 × 4 = **36 cm²**

Triangle:

**area = base × height ÷ 2**

A triangle with base 10 cm and height 6 cm has area:

10 × 6 ÷ 2 = **30 cm²**

## Volume: inside a box

**volume = length × width × height**

A 5 cm by 4 cm by 3 cm box has volume:

5 × 4 × 3 = **60 cm³**

Units reveal the question: cm is length, cm² is area, cm³ is volume.
