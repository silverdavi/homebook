## The idea

Protons name the element. Carbon is carbon because it has **6 protons**. That number does not change.

Neutrons are extra weight in the nucleus. They do not change the name. The common carbon atom has **6 neutrons**. A carbon atom with 8 neutrons is still carbon. It is a heavier carbon. That is an isotope: same protons, different neutrons.

Electrons in a neutral atom match the protons. Carbon: 6 protons, 6 electrons.

You do not need the group number today. The exam asks protons or neutrons for elements that have not been the daily card.

A few to have:

| Element | Protons | Neutrons (common) |
|---|---|---|
| Beryllium | 4 | 5 |
| Fluorine | 9 | 10 |
| Magnesium | 12 | 12 |
| Argon | 18 | 22 |

Argon is the odd one in that list: 18 protons, **22** neutrons, not 18.
