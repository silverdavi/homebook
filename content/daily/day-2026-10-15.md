## 1. Estimate before you calculate

An estimate tells you what neighborhood the answer should live in.

**47 × 6**

Round 47 to 50.  
50 × 6 = **300**. That is the estimate.

Now calculate exactly:

47 × 6 = (40 × 6) + (7 × 6) = 240 + 42 = **282**.

282 is close to 300, so it is believable. If you got 2,820 or 82, the estimate would warn you.

The estimate does not replace the exact answer. It checks it.

## 2. Mass number

An atom's mass number is:

**protons + neutrons**

- Carbon: 6 protons + 6 neutrons = mass number **12**
- Fluorine: 9 + 10 = **19**
- Sodium: 11 + 12 = **23**
- Argon: 18 + 22 = **40**

Electrons are almost weightless, so they are not part of the mass number.

The online exam asks for exact multiplication and proton or neutron counts. The estimation and mass-number work is how you understand and check those answers.
