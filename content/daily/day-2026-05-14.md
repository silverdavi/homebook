## What good thinking looks like

### Fraction subtraction

Compute **3/4 − 5/6**.

> LCM(4, 6) = 12.
> 3/4 = 9/12. 5/6 = 10/12.
> 9/12 − 10/12 = **−1/12**. Negative answer; the minus sign goes on the
> numerator.

Compute **7/9 − 1/3**.

> LCM(9, 3) = 9.
> 1/3 = 3/9.
> 7/9 − 3/9 = **4/9**. Already in lowest terms.

Full lesson: [lessons/fraction-sub](/daily/lessons/fraction-sub).

### Fraction multiplication

Compute **2/3 × 4/5**.

> Multiply tops, multiply bottoms.
> (2 × 4) / (3 × 5) = **8/15**. Already in lowest terms.

Compute **3/8 × 4/9**.

> Cancel before multiplying:
> 3 and 9 share a factor of 3 → 1 and 3.
> 4 and 8 share a factor of 4 → 1 and 2.
> Now (1/2) × (1/3) = **1/6**.

Full lesson: [lessons/fraction-mul](/daily/lessons/fraction-mul).
