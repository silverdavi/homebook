### The timeline of everything

From the **Big Bang (13.8 billion years ago)** to **you (Homo sapiens,
about 300,000 years ago)** — the whole story in five numbers. If the
entire history of the universe were one year, humans show up in the last
couple of minutes before midnight on December 31. That's the scale.

### The elements that make you and the stars

**Hydrogen** is the lightest thing there is and most of the universe.
**Helium** is the gas in the Sun. **Carbon** is the backbone of every
living thing. **Oxygen** is what you breathe. Same periodic table — but
these are the elements that *matter*.

### The treaties that drew the modern world

**Westphalia (1648)** literally invented the idea of a country.
**Versailles (1919)** was the peace deal so harsh it helped cause the next
war. **Good Friday (1998)** ended 30 years of bombing in Northern Ireland.
Paper that changed everything.
