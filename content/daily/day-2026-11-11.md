## Why there's an order

What is 5 + 2 × 3? Left to right gives 7 × 3 = 21. Multiplying first gives 5 + 6 = 11. Both can't be right, so mathematicians agreed on an order. **The answer is 11.**

## The order

1. **Brackets** ( ) first.
2. **× and ÷** next, left to right.
3. **+ and −** last, left to right.

## Examples

- 5 + 2 × 3 → multiply first: 2 × 3 = 6 → 5 + 6 = **11**.
- (5 + 2) × 3 → brackets first: 5 + 2 = 7 → 7 × 3 = **21**.
- 4 × 6 − 12 ÷ 3 → 4 × 6 = 24 and 12 ÷ 3 = 4 → 24 − 4 = **20**.

**Tip:** underline the × and ÷ parts first, work them out, then do the + and −.

| See this | Do this first |
| --- | --- |
| ( ) | inside the brackets |
| × or ÷ next to + or − | the × or ÷ |
| only + and − | left to right |

## World — Veterans Day and World War I

Reading time: about **11 minutes**. Every World question on the exam comes from here.

### Why today

Today is **Veterans Day**. World War I fighting stopped at **11 a.m. on November 11, 1918**: "the eleventh hour of the eleventh day of the eleventh month." In 1954, the US renamed the day to honor **everyone who has served in the military**. (Memorial Day, in May, honors those who died.)

### How it started

In June 1914, Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria-Hungary was shot in **Sarajevo**. Because of alliances, one shooting pulled in country after country. **World War I** lasted **1914–1918**.

### What it was like

On the **Western Front**, soldiers fought from **trenches across Belgium and France**. Machine guns, poison gas, and artillery made attacks deadly. The lines barely moved for years. About **20 million** people died.

It was a war of empires. **Over a million soldiers from India**, and many from Africa and the Caribbean, fought for empires that ruled them.

### What came after

- Four empires collapsed: the **Ottoman, Austro-Hungarian, Russian, and German**. New countries appeared, many with borders drawn by Britain and France.
- Countries formed the **League of Nations** to try to prevent another war. It failed to stop World War II, but the United Nations was built on its idea in 1945.

### Poppies

Red poppies grew on the battlefields of **Flanders, in Belgium**. A Canadian doctor, John McCrae, wrote the poem **"In Flanders Fields"** there in 1915. People wear poppies in November **to remember the soldiers who died**.
