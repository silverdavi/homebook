# Monday, November 9 — Day 44

**Volume is how much space is inside: length × width × height.**

Read this first (about **8 minutes**). It builds on Day 34: area is length × width.

## From area to volume

Area counts **squares** on a flat floor. Volume counts **cubes** that fill a box.

A box 4 cm long, 3 cm wide, and 2 cm tall:

1. The floor (base): 4 × 3 = **12** cubes.
2. Stack 2 layers: 12 × 2 = **24 cm³**.

**cm³** ("cubic centimeters") means cubes 1 cm on each side. 1 cm³ of water is 1 mL.

## A cube

All three sides are the same. A cube with 5 cm sides: 5 × 5 × 5 = **125 cm³**.

## Finding a missing side

A box holds 60 cm³. Its base is 5 cm by 3 cm. How tall?

1. Base: 5 × 3 = **15**.
2. Height: 60 ÷ 15 = **4 cm**.

| Question | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Volume of a box | length × width × height |
| Volume of a cube | side × side × side |
| Missing height | volume ÷ (length × width) |

## World — the Berlin Wall and the Cold War

Reading time: about **11 minutes**. Every World question on the exam comes from here.

**37 years ago today, on November 9, 1989, the Berlin Wall fell.**

### The Cold War

After World War II, the **United States** and the **Soviet Union** became rivals. They never fought each other directly, so it was called the **Cold War** (about 1947–1991). Instead they raced to build **nuclear weapons and rockets to space**, and backed opposite sides in wars in other countries.

Europe was split by what Winston Churchill called the **Iron Curtain**: Soviet-controlled **Eastern Europe** on one side, Western Europe on the other.

### A city cut in two

Germany was split into **East Germany** (Soviet side) and **West Germany**. Berlin, inside East Germany, was split too.

About 3 million East Germans fled west. So on the night of **August 13, 1961**, East Germany built a wall through **Berlin** to **stop its own people from escaping**. The government called it a defense, but the guards and guns faced inward. Families were cut off from each other. At least 140 people died trying to cross.

### How it fell

In 1989, East Germans held **peaceful protests**. In Leipzig, crowds grew to hundreds of thousands every Monday. On November 9, an official announced at a press conference, **by mistake**, that the border was open "immediately." Crowds rushed the gates, the guards let them through, and people began chipping the Wall apart.

The Wall stood **28 years**. Germany reunited in 1990, with **Berlin** as its capital. In 1991, the **Soviet Union** broke up into **15 countries**, including Russia, Ukraine, and Kazakhstan.

The lesson: ordinary people, protesting peacefully together, brought down a wall that armies couldn't.
