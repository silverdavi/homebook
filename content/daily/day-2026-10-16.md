## Fast anchors

- **50%** means half.
- **25%** means one quarter.
- **10%** means divide by 10.
- **5%** is half of 10%.
- **20%** is twice 10%.

## Example: 25% of 80

25% is one quarter.  
80 ÷ 4 = **20**.

## Example: 15% of 60

10% of 60 = 6.  
5% of 60 = 3.  
15% = 10% + 5%, so 6 + 3 = **9**.

## Discounts

A $40 shirt with a 25% discount:

25% of 40 = 10.  
The discount is $10.  
The new price is **$30**.

Read the question carefully: “How much is the discount?” and “What is the price after the discount?” are different questions.
