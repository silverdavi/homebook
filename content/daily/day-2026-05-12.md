## What good thinking looks like

### GCF

Find the GCF of **18 and 24**.

> Factors of 18: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18.
> Factors of 24: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24.
> Common: 1, 2, 3, **6**.
> GCF = **6**.

Find the GCF of **12 and 35**.

> Factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12.
> Factors of 35: 1, 5, 7, 35.
> Common: 1.
> GCF = **1**. (Coprime.)

Full lesson with Euclid's algorithm and more examples:
[lessons/gcf](/daily/lessons/gcf).

The Fractions Lab at [/fractions](/fractions) has an interactive GCF mode
if you'd rather practice that way.
