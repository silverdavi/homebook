## Valence, the short version

Outer electrons. Group tells you the number.

- Group 1 (H, Li, Na) = **1**
- Group 2 (He is the exception: **2**, not 8; Be, Mg = **2**)
- Then across the row: B **3**, C **4**, N **5**, O **6**, F **7**, Ne **8**
- Same pattern on row 3: Al **3**, Si **4**, P **5**, S **6**, Cl **7**, Ar **8**

Sodium (1) gives an electron to chlorine (7). That's table salt. You don't need more than that today.

## The multiply, one worked one

**17 × 25.**

Split: (10 + 7) × (20 + 5).

Four pieces: 10×20 = **200**, 10×5 = **50**, 7×20 = **140**, 7×5 = **35**.

Add them in that order: 200 + 50 = 250, + 140 = 390, + 35 = **425**.

Write the four numbers down. The slip is almost always the last add, not the idea.

## The two dates

- **Westphalia, 1648.** Often called the start of the idea that a country rules its own land.
- **Versailles, 1919.** Signed in the Hall of Mirrors. The terms were so hard that historians point at them as a cause of the next world war.

If you remember nothing else from the peace list today, remember those two years.
