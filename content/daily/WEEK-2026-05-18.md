Same content as week 1. Different shape.

Last week was the introduction. This week is where the skills get
real. We learned a few things together:

  0 / 4 on fraction addition to 4 / 6, and his GCF was clean. When
  the day owns *one* idea and gives him real practice, he gets it.

breaks → 3 hours of work). We expect more focus from Yerachmiel in
return. The exams stay at **22 questions** — last week's tighter
size is the new normal.
