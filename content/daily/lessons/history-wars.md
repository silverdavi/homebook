# 15 Wars and Their Years

For the trial we're learning **15 wars** and the **year each one started**.

We're using **start year**. Many of these wars also have a famous *end*
year (1945 for WWII, 1865 for the Civil War). Don't confuse them. The
exam asks for the **start**.

Tolerance on the exam is **±1 year**. So if the answer is 1914 you can
type 1913, 1914, or 1915 and get credit. (We're being generous on
purpose — the goal is *roughly when*, not the exact day.)

## A note on Vietnam

The Vietnam War's start year is genuinely fuzzy:

- **1954** — French withdrawal after Dien Bien Phu (start of the
  partition).
- **1955** — typical "official" US start (advisors arriving).
- **1959** — North Vietnam's communist party authorizes armed struggle.
- **1965** — large-scale US ground troops.

We're using **1955** with ±1 tolerance. If you write 1954 or 1956 you get
credit.

---

## How to memorize 15 dates

Group them. The dates fall into clusters:

- **Late 1700s / early 1800s — American era:** 1775, 1812.
- **Mid-1800s — slavery and expansion:** 1846, 1861.
- **Late 1800s — empire era:** 1870, 1898.
- **Early 1900s — modern war:** 1904, 1914, 1917.
- **1930s-50s — totalitarian era and Cold War start:** 1936, 1939, 1950,
  1955.
- **Late 20th–early 21st — modern conflicts:** 1990, 2003.

If you can place a war in its cluster, you're already within ±1.
