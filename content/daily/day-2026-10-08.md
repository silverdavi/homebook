## The idea

One line, big on the left, us on the right. The number is how many **millions of years ago**.

| What | Million years ago |
|---|---|
| Big Bang | 13800 |
| Earth forms | 4540 |
| First life | 3700 |
| Cambrian explosion | 540 |
| First fish | 520 |
| First land plants | 470 |
| First dinosaurs | 230 |
| First mammals | 210 |
| Asteroid, dinosaurs gone | 66 |
| First hominids | 7 |
| Homo sapiens | 0.3 |

Fish come just after the Cambrian. Mammals come just after dinosaurs. Humans are the last mark, almost now.

The exam takes an answer within about 10 percent. 520 and 500 are the same idea. 0.3 can be answered as 0.

A few questions are yesterday’s multiply, so the last addition does not disappear.
