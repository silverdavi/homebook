**A data set has a center and a spread.**

The complete clock is above. This reading takes about **10 minutes**.

Use this set:

**3, 5, 5, 7, 10**

## Mean

Add everything, then divide by how many numbers there are.

3 + 5 + 5 + 7 + 10 = 30  
30 ÷ 5 = **6**

## Median

Put the values in order and take the middle one.

The middle of 3, 5, **5**, 7, 10 is **5**.

With an even number of values, average the middle two.

## Range

Largest minus smallest:

10 − 3 = **7**

Mean, median, and range answer different questions. Do not guess which one the exam asks for.
