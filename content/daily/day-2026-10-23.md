**A data set has a center and a spread.**

The complete clock is above. This reading takes about **10 minutes**.

Use this set:

**3, 5, 5, 7, 10**

## Mean

Add everything, then divide by how many numbers there are.

3 + 5 + 5 + 7 + 10 = 30
30 ÷ 5 = **6**

## Median

Put the values in order and take the middle one.

The middle of 3, 5, **5**, 7, 10 is **5**.

With an even number of values, average the middle two.

## Range

Largest minus smallest:

10 − 3 = **7**

Mean, median, and range answer different questions. Do not guess which one the exam asks for.

## World block — four current crises

This part takes about **12 minutes**. These notes are current as of **October 2026**.

- **Ukraine, Eastern Europe:** Russia's full-scale invasion began in 2022. Attacks continue to kill civilians and damage homes, power, water, transport, schools, and health facilities.
- **Sudan, northeastern Africa:** war between the Sudanese Armed Forces and Rapid Support Forces began in 2023. Sudan is now the world's largest hunger and displacement crisis.
- **Gaza, on the eastern Mediterranean:** a 2025 ceasefire improved some access, but most people still need shelter and food assistance. Funding cuts are reducing aid.
- **Myanmar, Southeast Asia:** conflict, forced displacement, food insecurity, and climate disasters overlap.

Governments and armed groups tell competing stories. That does not make every claim equally supported. Check sources, distinguish civilians from governments or armed groups, and look for who holds power.

A progressive starting point is consistent: protect civilians, oppose collective punishment, defend human rights and self-determination, and insist that humanitarian aid reach people who need it.
