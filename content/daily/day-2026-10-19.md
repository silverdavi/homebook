**Unit rate: make the bottom number 1.**

The complete clock is above. This reading takes about **8 minutes**.

## Example: miles per hour

A car travels 180 miles in 3 hours.

180 ÷ 3 = **60 miles per hour**.

The word **per** tells you to divide.

## Example: price per item

4 notebooks cost $12.

12 ÷ 4 = **$3 per notebook**.

## Compare fairly

One store sells 6 cans for $9. Another sells 4 cans for $7.

- Store A: 9 ÷ 6 = **$1.50 per can**
- Store B: 7 ÷ 4 = **$1.75 per can**

Store A is cheaper even though its total price is higher. Unit rate lets you compare equal amounts.

Always write the unit: miles **per hour**, dollars **per notebook**, words **per minute**.
