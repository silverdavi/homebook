## History — Abraham Accords + the through-line

| Accord | Year |
|---|---|
| Abraham Accords | 2020 |

Today's review pulls earlier accords too — Versailles, Oslo,
Westphalia.

The Abraham Accords (2020) normalized relations between Israel and
the UAE, Bahrain, later Morocco and Sudan. Notable for what they
**didn't** do: they didn't address the Palestinian question. The
deal worked around it. Many commentators — and the Palestinians
themselves — see this as the Versailles-style problem all over
again: a "peace" arranged over the heads of the people whose lives
are at stake.

The through-line of this week:
- Westphalia worked because it set rules everyone bought into.
- Versailles failed because the losers had no stake in the outcome.
- Oslo failed because Palestinians were promised statehood and got
  more occupation.
- Abraham Accords sidelined the Palestinian question entirely.

**Who's at the table** turns out to matter as much as **what's
signed**.
