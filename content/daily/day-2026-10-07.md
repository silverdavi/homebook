## The idea

Split the second number into tens and ones. Multiply. Then add. The miss is stopping before that add.

**24 × 13.**

13 is 10 + 3.

24 × 10 = 240.

24 × 3 = 72.

240 + 72 = **312**.

**18 × 7.**

18 is 10 + 8.

10 × 7 = 70.

8 × 7 = 56.

70 + 56 = **126**.

Write both pieces. Add them. That sum is the answer.
