## The one method

To add or subtract, the denominators have to match.

**1/2 + 1/3.**

Halves and thirds meet at sixths. 1/2 = 3/6. 1/3 = 2/6. Add the tops: **5/6**.

**3/4 − 1/6.**

Fourths and sixths meet at twelfths. 3/4 = 9/12. 1/6 = 2/12. Subtract: **7/12**.

Multiply is different. You do not find a common denominator. Cancel first if you can, then multiply tops and bottoms. **2/3 × 3/8 = 1/4**, because the 3 cancels.

Divide is flip the second fraction, then multiply. **1/2 ÷ 1/3 = 1/2 × 3/1 = 3/2.**

Every answer in lowest terms.
