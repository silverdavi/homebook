## The whole trial, in one pass

**Math.** Greatest common factor and least common multiple of two numbers. Then fractions: add, subtract, multiply, divide, and the inverse (flip it). Answers in lowest terms. Then times tables through 15×15, and the breakdown for bigger products: split into tens and ones, multiply the four pieces, add them last and slowly.

**Elements, H through Ar.** Protons, neutrons of the common isotope, electrons in the neutral atom, and valence — the outer electrons. Valence follows the group: H = 1, He = 2, then 1 through 8 across each row. You were usually one or two off. That's a counting slip. Read the group number.

**Fifteen wars, the year they started** (±1 year). The ones to have cold: American Revolution **1775**, Civil War **1861**, World War I **1914**, World War II **1939**, Korea **1950**.

**Peace dates** (±2 years). The two that cost points: Westphalia **1648**, Versailles **1919**. Also Good Friday **1998**, Oslo **1993**.

**Deep time, in millions of years ago.** Earth **4,540**. First life **3,700**. Cambrian explosion **540**. The asteroid **66**. Us, Homo sapiens, **0.3**.

A few that are just true, so the review isn't only a list:

- Sharks are older than trees.
- T. rex lived closer in time to you than to Stegosaurus.
- Helium was found in the Sun before anyone found it on Earth.
- Korea's 1953 deal was a cease-fire. The two Koreas never signed a peace.
