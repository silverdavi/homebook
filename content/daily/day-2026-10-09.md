## The idea

LCM means least common multiple. List the multiples of each number. The answer is the **first number that shows up in both lists**.

**4 and 6.**

4, 8, **12**, 16, …

6, **12**, 18, …

They meet at **12**.

**5 and 7.**

They meet at **35**. Neither is a multiple of the other, so the meeting point is their product.

If one number is already a multiple of the other, that number is the LCM. **5 and 15** meet at **15**.

GCF is the largest number that divides both. It goes down. LCM goes up. The exam says LCM on every one of these.
