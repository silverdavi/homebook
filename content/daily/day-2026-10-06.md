## The idea

Valence is the electrons on the outside. It is not the proton count. Sodium has 11 protons and **1** valence electron.

Same column, same valence. Across row 2 or row 3 the numbers go **1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8**.

| Column | Examples | Valence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lithium, sodium | 1 |
| 2 | Beryllium, magnesium | 2 |
| 3 | Boron, aluminum | 3 |
| 4 | Carbon, silicon | 4 |
| 5 | Nitrogen, phosphorus | 5 |
| 6 | Oxygen, sulfur | 6 |
| 7 | Fluorine, chlorine | 7 |
| 8 | Neon, argon | 8 |

Helium is the exception. Its shell is full at **2**, not 8. Hydrogen is 1.

A few questions are divide-by-flipping: flip the second fraction, then multiply. **1/2 ÷ 1/4 = 2.**
