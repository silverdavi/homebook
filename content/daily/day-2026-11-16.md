## The four rules

| Day | Rule | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday: volume | length × width × height | 4 × 3 × 2 = 24 cm³ |
| Monday: missing height | volume ÷ base | 60 ÷ (5 × 3) = 4 cm |
| Tuesday: equations | do the opposite to both sides | x − 3 = 8 → x = 11 |
| Thursday: order | brackets, then × ÷, then + − | 5 + 2 × 3 = 11 |
| Friday: speed | D = S × T, S = D ÷ T, T = D ÷ S | 240 km in 4 h = 60 km/h |

## The usual mistakes

- **Volume:** adding the sides instead of multiplying them.
- **Equations:** doing the opposite to only one side. Check by putting x back in.
- **Order:** going left to right through a × or ÷. Underline the × and ÷ first.
- **Speed:** dividing the wrong way. Cover the one you want in the triangle.

- **Friday, refugees:** a refugee **fled danger and can't go back**. The **1951 Convention** promised **not to send refugees back to danger**. Most refugees live in **poorer countries next door**.
