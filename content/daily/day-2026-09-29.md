## The four

- **Mexican-American War, 1846.** The United States and Mexico fought over land that is now the southwest of the United States, including California and Texas as a border fight. It ended in 1848.
- **Spanish-American War, 1898.** Short. The United States fought Spain. It took the Philippines, Puerto Rico, and Guam. Cuba left Spanish rule.
- **Gulf War, 1990.** Iraq invaded Kuwait in August 1990. A large coalition pushed Iraq back out in early 1991. This is not the Iraq War.
- **Iraq War, 2003.** A different war, thirteen years later. The United States invaded Iraq itself. Do not trade this year with 1990.
